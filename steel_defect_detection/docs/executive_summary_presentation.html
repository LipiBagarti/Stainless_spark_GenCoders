🏭 Business Context & Manufacturing Problem
  • Strategic Quality Focus: Surface quality is paramount in stainless steel. Jindal Stainless focuses on improving product quality, manufacturing efficiency and customer satisfaction through digital technologies.
  • Late Defect Detection: Scratches, scale, roll marks & edge cracks detected late cause yield loss, costly downgrades, rework, and customer rejections.
  • Manual Inspection Bottleneck: Visual inspection is slow, subjective, fatigue-prone, and cannot scale to 15–20 m/s mill speeds.
✨ GenCoders High-Accuracy AI Cascade
  • Tier 1 Multi-Model Detectors: YOLOv8 (Speed) + Faster R-CNN (Anchor Precision) + RT-DETR (Global Vision Transformer).
  • Weighted Boxes Fusion (WBF): Consensus box averaging with Multi-Model Agreement scoring (1–3 models).
  • Tier 2 Fine Verifier: EfficientNet-B0 (3,332 ground-truth crops) rejects glare & surface oil noise.

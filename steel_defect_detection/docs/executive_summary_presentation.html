🔴 The Industrial Manufacturing Bottleneck
  • Continuous High-Speed Mill Speeds: Strip moves at 15–20 m/s requiring inference latency under 20 ms (>60 FPS).
  • High False Alarm Penalty: Hallucinated defects halt rolling lines or cause unnecessary prime coil downgrades costing millions annually.
  • Complex Surface Topography: Surface oil, optical glare, scale, and subtle inter-class variance (crazing vs inclusions vs scratches) confound single models.
✨ GenCoders Solution: Two-Tier Multi-Model Cascade
  • Tier 1 Multi-Detector Ensemble: YOLOv8 (Single-Stage Speed) + Faster R-CNN ResNet50 (Anchor Precision) + RT-DETR (Global Attention).
  • Weighted Boxes Fusion (WBF): Replaces destructive NMS with confidence-weighted spatial box consensus and multi-model agreement scoring.
  • Tier 2 Fine-Grained Verifier: EfficientNet-B0 verifies cropped defect textures to suppress reflection and oil noise.
